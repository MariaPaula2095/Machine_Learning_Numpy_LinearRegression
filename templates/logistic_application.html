Problem Context & Q&A
What is the problem?

E-commerce sites want to anticipate whether a visitor is likely to buy, so they can trigger timely offers or support instead of guessing blindly.

What phenomenon is being analyzed?

The relationship between the time a visitor spends browsing product pages and whether that session ends in a completed purchase.

What do the data represent?

Each record is one browsing session: the time spent on product pages ({{ independent_var_unit }}) and whether it ended in a purchase.

Why apply logistic regression?

Because the outcome is binary (purchase / no purchase), logistic regression estimates the probability of purchase from historical data.

What does the model predict?

Whether a session with a given {{ independent_var_name }} will result in {{ class_1 }} or {{ class_0 }}.
