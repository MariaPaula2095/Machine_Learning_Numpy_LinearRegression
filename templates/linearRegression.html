Problem Context & Q&A
What is the problem?

Farmers need to know, before applying fertilizer, how much it will actually improve their harvest. Without a data-driven estimate, amounts are often chosen by intuition, wasting resources or under-fertilizing.

What phenomenon is being analyzed?

The relationship between the amount of fertilizer applied to a crop and the resulting crop yield, assuming a linear trend between the two.

What do the data represent?

Each record represents one crop/field observation: the fertilizer amount applied ({{ independent_var_unit }}) and the yield obtained ({{ dependent_var_unit }}) for that observation.

Why apply linear regression?

To model the trend between fertilizer and yield from historical data, so it can be used to estimate yield for fertilizer amounts that were not directly observed.

What value does the model predict?

The expected {{ dependent_var_name }} ({{ dependent_var_unit }}) for a given {{ independent_var_name }} ({{ independent_var_unit }}) entered by the user.
