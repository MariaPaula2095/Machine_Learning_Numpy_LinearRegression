Phase 3: R2A2

K-Means Clustering Simulation

Manual 3-step iterations, euclidean distance calculation, centroid updating, and WCSS variance analysis.

1. Dataset Overview (100 Records)

The manual simulation evaluates 100 Spotify tracks (`spotify_100_tracks.csv`) using two numerical variables: Danceability ($X$) and Energy ($Y$) scaled between $[0, 1]$. Initial centroids were placed at $C_1=(0.10, 0.10)$, $C_2=(0.50, 0.50)$, and $C_3=(0.90, 0.90)$ to demonstrate convergence.

3. Iteration Calculations & Cluster Assignment

For each track, the Euclidean distance to each of the 3 centroids was computed as $d = \sqrt{(x - c_x)^2 + (y - c_y)^2}$, and the track was assigned to the nearest one. 4. Within-Cluster Variance (Inertia) Analysis
Variance Reduction Principle

The within-cluster sum of squares (WCSS) measures spatial compactness:

  • Iteration 1: $WCSS = 2.4440$ (Initial centroid placement)
  • Iteration 2: $WCSS = 1.0300$ (Rapid minimization)
  • Iteration 3: $WCSS = 1.0300$ (Convergence stability)

The sharp drop between Iteration 1 and 2 shows the centroids moving away from their arbitrary starting points toward the real density of the data. The stabilization from Iteration 2 to 3 confirms that centroids reached equilibrium and cluster membership stopped changing — i.e., the algorithm had already converged by Iteration 2.

5. Final Results & Cluster Interpretation

After 3 iterations the algorithm converged: cluster assignments stopped changing and the within-cluster variance stabilized at 1.03. The table below summarizes each final cluster and what it represents in terms of track mood.

Cluster 1 · 30 tracks
Low Energy, Low Danceability

Final centroid: (0.2849, 0.2542)

Calm, low-tempo tracks with little rhythmic drive — the kind of songs suited to relaxing, focusing, or winding down rather than dancing.

Cluster 2 · 35 tracks
High Danceability, Moderate Energy

Final centroid: (0.7368, 0.4479)

Groove-driven tracks that are easy to dance to without being intense — think mid-tempo pop or R&B, well suited for casual listening or background playlists with a beat.

Cluster 3 · 35 tracks
High Energy, High Danceability

Final centroid: (0.6524, 0.8213)

The most intense and upbeat group — high-energy, danceable tracks typical of party playlists, workout mixes, or high-tempo electronic/pop music.

Overall interpretation: the manual K-Means run separated the 100 tracks into three musically meaningful mood groups — calm (Cluster 1), moderately danceable (Cluster 2), and high-energy/party (Cluster 3) — without ever being told what "mood" means. This is exactly the value of unsupervised clustering: it found structure directly from the numerical relationship between danceability and energy.
